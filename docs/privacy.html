Duo Tanks Privacy Policy

Last updated October 18, 2026

Duo Tanks does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

What the app uses

Your game settings (such as world, rounds and volume) are saved on your device so they're remembered next time. They never leave your device.

The app reads your phone's hinge angle and orientation to control the game. This happens on your device and is not recorded or sent anywhere.

What the app doesn't do

No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no network connections.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue at github.com/AKloc/duo-tanks/issues.
