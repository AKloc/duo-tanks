Duo Tanks

A turn-based artillery game for one or two players. Aim, account for the wind, and blow your opponent off the map.

Support

Found a bug or have a question? Open an issue on GitHub.

Tips

  • Hold the FIRE button to charge your shot; release to fire.
  • On iPhone Duo, half-open the phone and open or close the hinge to aim.
  • Buried? Fire a Dig Charge straight up.
