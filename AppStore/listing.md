## Description (4,000)
Duo Tanks is a classic turn-based artillery game. Angle your cannon, charge your shot, account for the wind, and blow your opponent off the map.

DESTRUCTIBLE BATTLEFIELDS
Every blast carves the ground. Dig a crater under your rival and watch them fall, bury them under a landslide, or flood a valley with mud.

20 WEAPONS
From the trusty Shell to MIRVs, Nukes, Napalm, Rollers that seek out valleys, Burrowers that tunnel under hills, and a Laser that cuts straight through them. Earn money every round and spend it in the shop on weapons, shields, parachutes, repair kits and fuel.

PLAY YOUR WAY
• Two players on one phone, with private turns so your opponent can't see your shopping
• Or against the computer on Easy, Normal or Hard
• Multi-round matches with a running scoreboard

BUILT FOR FOLDABLE IPHONE
On a foldable iPhone, half-open the phone and use the hinge to aim. While a shot is in flight, the top half follows the shell and the bottom half shows the whole battlefield. Lay it flat between two players for face-to-face tabletop play.

FOUR WORLDS
Moonlit hills, desert dusk, snowy nights, and the Moon itself, with low gravity and the occasional UFO. Rain, snow and lightning storms keep every round different.

THE DETAILS
A slow-motion kill cam for every finishing blow. Music that builds as the fight gets close. Haptics you can feel when you charge a shot.

No ads. No tracking. No in-app purchases.
